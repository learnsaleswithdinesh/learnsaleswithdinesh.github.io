Programs

How a program with me runs

  1. Week 0

    Needs discovery

    A short call and a few conversations with your team to find the gap that matters most, so we train for results and not for attendance.

  2. Day 1

    Workshop

    An interactive session built on your team's real cases: role-plays, practice and feedback, with very little lecturing.

  3. Weeks 1–4

    Practice & reinforcement

    Short follow-up tasks and check-ins so the skills turn into habits instead of fading by Friday.

  4. Week 6

    Review

    We look at what changed on the job and agree on the next step for your team.
