How a program runs

From diagnosis to on-the-job change

A typical 6-week journey. Short programs follow the same logic, compressed.

Week 0

Needs discovery

A short call and conversations with the team to find the one gap that matters most.

Day 1

Workshop

Role-plays, practice and feedback built on your team's real customer situations.

Weeks 1–4

Practice & reinforce

Short follow-up tasks, WhatsApp nudges and manager check-ins to build the habit.

Week 6

Review

Check what changed on the job and agree on next steps with the business head.

Session design

At least half of every session is practice.

My rule of thumb for a classroom day: about 30% concept, 50% practice and role-play, 20% action planning. People leave with words they've already said out loud.

  1. Real scripts, not slogansExact sentences for openings, objections and closes, in your team's language.
  2. Role-play with feedbackStructured practice in pairs and trios, with observer checklists.
  3. Manager toolkitA one-page guide so team leaders can coach the skill after the session.
